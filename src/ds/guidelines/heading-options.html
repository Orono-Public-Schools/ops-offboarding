Card heading options

Every card on the portal currently shouts in uppercase at 0.14em — “YOUR OPEN REQUESTS”, “FILE SOMETHING”, “WHO’S FILING”. Read down the home screen and it is four shouts before you reach anything you can do. These are four ways to fix that.

Turn 6 — card headings

Same card, same content. The question is how much voice a heading needs when the card below it already explains itself.

6a Uppercase strip — current
Your open requests2 open
Two requests, moving.

13px, 600, 0.14em, in a tinted strip. Unambiguously official — and unambiguously loud when four of them stack.

6b Sentence case, no strip

Your open requests

Both are moving. Nothing needs you today.

2 open
Two requests, moving.

16px semibold, sentence case, with room for a line of plain language underneath. Sounds like the rest of the product’s voice rather than a filing cabinet. Loses the “official” signal the brief asked for.

6c Eyebrow + title
Requests

Two are moving right now

Two requests, moving.

Keeps the uppercase letterspacing but demotes it to a small category label, and lets the real heading say something. Best of both — costs a line of vertical space per card.

6d Strip with a rule mark

Your open requests

2 open
Two requests, moving.

The current strip, plus a 3px gradient mark to anchor the left edge. Smallest change; adds a little brand without touching the type.
